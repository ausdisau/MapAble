# Life Twin, Support Simulation and Access Commons

## Life Twin

Life Twin records describe the participant’s support environment rather than a
diagnosis or score. Every domain record exposes source, verification status,
consent scopes, expiry, version and dispute state. Inferences remain
unverified, temporary and removable; they cannot be used for consequential
decisions until participant confirmation.

## Support simulation

The simulation endpoint is disabled by default and is deterministic. It shows
separate trade-offs, assumptions, uncertainty, safeguards and a human-review
requirement. It makes no clinical, discharge-readiness, eligibility, funding
or operational decision.

## Disability Access Commons

Commons contribution contracts are opt-in and define category, coarse
geographic precision, retention and research/advocacy/model-evaluation use
separately. No public participant data, single-report provider accusation, or
public dashboard is enabled in this increment. Any aggregate publishing
requires minimum-cell suppression, re-identification review, dispute process
and disabled-participant-majority governance approval.

## Decision register

The public-decision-register contract requires system purpose, data
categories, authority, oversight, risks, prohibited uses, tests, version
history, complaint pathway and rollback process before a future system can be
published.
