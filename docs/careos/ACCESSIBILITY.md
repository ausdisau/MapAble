# Accessibility

CareOS uses the MapAble dashboard shell, tokens and button primitives. Its
forms have visible labels, keyboard operation, visible focus, 44px targets,
semantic headings and live status messages. The experience reflows at text
zoom and offers plain-language wording and a standard non-AI path.

No status depends on colour alone. Errors and consent prompts preserve form
state. Future Easy Read and AAC rendering are represented in the context
contract and require participant-facing content review before expansion.
