# MapAble Competitive Position

**Status:** strategy documentation — not a production readiness claim  
**Inspection baseline:** main after productisation #327

## Positioning

MapAble is **participant-controlled infrastructure** that connects providers, workers,
transport, environments, equipment, employment, evidence, payment and service recovery
into one reliable journey.

MapAble is **not** merely:

- another support-worker marketplace;
- another provider directory;
- another rostering product;
- another accessibility map;
- another plan-management app;
- another generic AI assistant.

## Competitive chain

```
participant goal
→ authority and consent
→ Communication Passport
→ provider and worker readiness
→ service agreement
→ accessible transport
→ environmental preflight
→ equipment dependencies
→ service delivery
→ participant review
→ evidence-backed billing
→ outcome
→ recovery when something fails
```

## Competitor category summary

| Group | Typical strength | MapAble response |
|-------|------------------|------------------|
| Large registered providers | Employed workforce, supervision, complex support | Partner for delivery; own portability, access intelligence, journey recovery |
| Support marketplaces | Liquidity, booking, mobile | Own journey + evidence + environmental fit; reject rating/pay-to-rank |
| Provider-management SaaS | Rostering, notes, billing | Provider Ops as read-only attention; participant remains authority |
| Accessibility / transport platforms | Maps, routes, schedules | Personal fit, freshness, worker readiness, evidence-to-payment, recovery |

## Defensibility rules

- Participant choice and control come first.
- Relationship ≠ authority; supporter ≠ nominee; administrator ≠ ambient access.
- Consent is purpose-, recipient-, field-bound and revocable.
- Functional requirements must not be inferred from diagnosis.
- Unknown ≠ accessible; stale ≠ current; course completion ≠ competency.
- Candidate ≠ available ≠ compatible ≠ participant approved ≠ confirmed.
- Service delivered ≠ outcome; payment ≠ quality.
- Feature flag enabled ≠ production approval.
- AI may explain, draft, compare, simulate and propose — never approve workers,
  claims, payments, consent, safeguarding closure, clinical decisions or regulatory submissions.
- No automatic worker assignment; no participant/worker quality scores; no pay-to-rank.
- No smartphone-only essential service; no sale of participant data; no advertising from journeys.
- Participants can export, pause, reject, dispute, appeal and request a human.
- Maps and graphs must have complete list alternatives.
