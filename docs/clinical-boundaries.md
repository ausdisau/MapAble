# Clinical and safeguarding boundaries

CareOS may retrieve authorised support-plan versions, explain operational
steps, identify missing evidence, prepare questions and route review.

CareOS must not diagnose, prescribe, change medication or treatment, authorise
restrictive practices, decide emergency treatment, determine capacity, or make
safeguarding findings.

Support-plan versions retain author, authority, effective/review dates,
limitations and supersession history. Workers receive only the current,
minimum-necessary instructions for a confirmed service.

High-intensity worker eligibility requires current verified competency evidence
and participant-specific orientation where required. Missing, expired or
revoked evidence produces `requires_human_review`; it never becomes an
automatic assignment.

Safeguarding and clinical review records are human-owned queues. Existing
incident and complaint services remain the canonical record systems.
