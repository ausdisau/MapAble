# CareOS Kernel safety case

The linked supported-appointment journey is synthetic and capped at L2
recommendation authority. The deterministic policy engine evaluates every
proposed action. Confirmation tokens bind an actor, participant, capability,
action, exact payload hash, policy version and expiry, and are one-time-use.

No production executor is registered. The journey cannot create care requests,
transport trips, messages, payments, cancellations, allocations or emergency
actions. Missing evidence requires clarification or human review.

Safeguarding, clinical, capacity, funding and emergency decisions remain
human-only. `MAPABLE_CAREOS_ENABLED=false` immediately disables new CareOS
requests while standard MapAble service paths remain available.
