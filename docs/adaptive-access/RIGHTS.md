# Participant rights (Adaptive Access Network)

## Control

- The participant defines goals, presentation preferences, and disclosure.
- The participant may reject, pause, correct, revoke, or reverse.
- Delegates use their own identities; relationship does not equal authority.
- A supporter may assist without replacing the participant.
- The participant can request a human.
- No essential pathway may require AI, a smartphone, biometrics, voice, camera, QR code, or smart-home equipment.

## State honesty

Preference ≠ consent. Presentation adaptation ≠ meaning alteration.
Draft ≠ decision. Recommendation ≠ approval.
Credential verified ≠ suitability. Device online ≠ safe.
Sensor signal ≠ accessibility proof. Route found ≠ usable journey.
Simulation passed ≠ production safety.

## AI ceiling

AI may explain, translate, simplify, compare, summarise, retrieve, identify gaps, draft questions, simulate non-executable options, and prepare accessible renditions.

AI must not independently alter meaning, make a participant decision, infer consent or capacity, assign workers, book/cancel services, modify funding, approve payment, certify accessibility, command physical devices, contact emergency services, publish participant information, authorise delegates, issue government credentials, or determine legal/clinical outcomes.
