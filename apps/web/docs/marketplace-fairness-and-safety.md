# Marketplace fairness and safety

- Participants are not sales leads.
- Paid or sponsored status is not a comparison factor.
- Provider evidence is separated into verified records, provider statements,
  capacity observations and unknown information.
- Comparisons do not use disability, diagnosis, support intensity or protected
  characteristics as desirability signals.
- No provider is labelled “best” without participant-selected criteria.
- Participants can hide providers, remove shortlists and use the standard
  provider finder.
- Provider contact, disclosure, proposals, agreements and booking are disabled
  in this tranche.
- Complaints, privacy concerns and safeguarding reports remain human-reviewed.

Feature flags fail closed. Automatic provider selection, automatic agreement
acceptance and sponsored ranking are hard-disabled in code.
