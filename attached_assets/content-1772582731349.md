> Australian Disability is committed to promoting equity in Australian society for individuals with disabilities. We invite you to collaborate with us in our mission to build a more inclusive environment where everyone can flourish.

Aims and Purpose:

- To support the development of new technologies that improve the quality of life and the independence of persons living with a disability

- To provide persons with a disability with a forum through which they can express their views on government policies and other matters of relevance to their disability

- To support and enable fair and unbiased media representation of disability-related issues, through the development of news and original content

- To provide community-wide education on disability-related issues so as to reduce the instances of discrimination suffered by persons with a disability

- To establish an online community that encourages greater interaction between disabled and able-bodied people

- To promote the concept of ‘disability’ as a strong, healthy form of identity.

## Our Values

Main Content

Australian Disability is dedicated to enhancing the quality of life and independence of individuals with disabilities through innovative technology. We provide a platform for those affected by disability to voice their opinions on government policies and relevant issues. commitment to fair media representation ensures that diverse perspectives are shared through original content. By fostering an online, we aim to promote interaction and understanding between disabled and able-bodied individuals, celebrating disability as a healthy aspect of identity.
